# Trade pricing and evidence — v1.336.0

The live DFL search labeled Amon-Ra St. Brown + Tucker Kraft for CeeDee Lamb a Steal despite valuing the incoming side at 97 and the outgoing side at 141.8. The classifier allowed a lineup-impact advantage to substitute for winning player value. Recipient-specific bench discounts also made asset prices change with the destination.

Player values now use remaining-season points above a positional replacement baseline from the full available NFL projection feed. The baseline is the league-size × 3 rank for RB/WR and × 1.5 for QB/TE; a small test/fallback universe uses 55% of the best positional pace. Surplus is normalized against the largest available surplus with exponent 1.25. ADP carries at most 16% weight and declines as current games accumulate; add/drop activity retains its bounded 2% adjustment. These are transparent model assumptions, not externally calibrated market prices. Availability is already included in forward points; it no longer gets a second value discount. The same asset price applies on every roster. Fit, usable depth, lineup effects and required cuts remain separate.

A Steal requires at least a 12% incoming value advantage, incoming lower sensitivity value above outgoing upper sensitivity value, supported inputs, no current injury or stale/fallback flag, and no material loss to the user's useful roster. Sensitivity ranges depend on observed scoring variability/sample size, with wider assumptions for fallback, injury and stale data. They are not confidence intervals or calibrated probabilities. A comparable same-position forecast within 15%, plus another rated piece worth at least 5, is flagged as a premium for a similar player. It cannot qualify as a Steal. Paying a significant premium cannot be hidden by a large lineup gain in the ACCEPT stamp.

All completed weekly results are fetched through the preceding week; the ongoing week is excluded from recent form and consistency. Bye/missing games do not count as played zeroes; explicit played zero-point games do. Selected-player evidence shows DFL points per game, recent scoring, observed 25th/75th percentile results, targets/carries, NFL team, current opponent, injury designation and practice participation. Usage and the opponent are context, not independent additive scoring bonuses. Availability and completed-results fetch timestamps are separate, and source failures are disclosed.

The two-team and directed group evaluators, offers, counteroffers, saved proposals and share-card totals all use these prices. Directed trades compare each owner's actual outgoing package rather than assuming the second member received everything. Share-card designs remain unchanged. New completed-trade receipts carry `dflyzer-trade-v3-player-value` and cannot reverse the player-value winner solely through roster fit. Older frozen receipts retain their original interpretation. Unmodeled K/DEF roster slots survive skill-player cuts.

Expert consensus is **not connected** and is explicitly labeled in the app. Sleeper projections and draft ADP are not an expert ROS feed. FantasyPros' official data service provides ROS consensus/projections, but production access requires a licensed API key: https://www.fantasypros.com/api-data/ and https://api.fantasypros.com/public/v2/docs/. No paid subscription was created, key was exposed, or sample data presented as live. With access supplied, the next integration must store the key on the server, validate season/scoring/ROS scope and source update time, join players through verified IDs (or unique name/position/team), show source/expert coverage and opinion spread, and fall back explicitly when stale. A scrape or one-time opinion pasted into source code is not a live feed.

Future NFL matchups/playoff schedules, offensive-line/QB changes, exact injury return dates and acceptance probability are not included in this release. No change claims perfect trade accuracy. Completed production is historical evidence; forecast gains still apply only to the receiving owner's unplayed season.

The rotated decorative offer arrow is constrained to its own width and does not intercept Analyze clicks on phones. The observed-outcome percentage is labeled as package direction rather than calibrated model accuracy.

Validation includes regression tests for the reported comparable-star package, value loss versus lineup gain, sensitivity overlap, injury/stale gates, identical prices across recipients, directed multi-member asset accounting, point-gap pricing and observed consistency. The production Trade browser review covers 320/390/768px routing, share cards, saved proposals, reload, counters, stale ownership and expanded evidence. A separate read-only browser uses current DFL rosters to verify zero false Steal offers for the Amon-Ra/CeeDee target, both anchor selectors, alternative offer analysis and legible player evidence.
