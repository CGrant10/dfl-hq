# Position-aware PPR trade prices — v1.380.0

The 12-team 1QB model omitted league-wide flex demand when pricing positions. It used RB36 / WR36 / TE18 / QB18 as replacement estimates regardless of which players would fill the league's twelve RB/WR/TE flex slots. This understated usable PPR receivers relative to a one-slot position such as tight end.

The model now fills dedicated demand first (12 QBs, 24 RBs, 24 WRs, 12 TEs), then selects the twelve highest availability-adjusted forecasts remaining across RB/WR/TE. Each selected flex adds demand only to its own position. Replacement retains the existing depth allowance of half the dedicated starting slots, plus that position's share of flex demand. Full NFL projection coverage, including unrostered players, determines these baselines; roster ownership and duplicate player entries do not change them. This is a league replacement estimate, not a claim about who is available on waivers. Sparse feeds retain the existing conservative forecast fallback. The live Sleeper lineup is verified as QB / RB / RB / WR / WR / TE / FLEX / K / DEF; K and DEF are outside offensive trade valuation.

Fresh, uniquely matched FantasyPros PPR rest-of-season **overall** consensus now contributes 50% of asset value, with the production/scarcity model contributing the other 50%. This supersedes the earlier 15% weighting documented in `trade-expert-confidence-validation.md`. The existing smooth rank-to-value index is an internal opinion index, not a published FantasyPros trade price. Positional expert ranks remain visible and drive disagreement checks. The 1QB streamer discount also applies to expert values; only an earned elite weekly edge retains full QB pricing. Stale, missing, mismatched-season, mismatched-scoring and ambiguous expert inputs remain excluded or unmatched. Injuries, sample size, actual PPR scoring, consistency, expert disagreement and source freshness retain their existing review safeguards. No name-specific exceptions or blanket TE penalties were added.
