# Broadcast compositions and forward trade model — v1.333.0

Home keeps the Bring the Receipts opener and the existing DFL artwork. Champion and Chip Eater subjects are larger, nearly opaque and contained above the controls. Trades show real player portraits and label value balance. Archive stories use one main number, readable supporting text and a subject-specific image. Rivalries show both teams and the series rather than oversized handles. Records format small decimal margins consistently.

Current awards, live stories, pinned items and commissioner-authored/featured content stay in the deck. Historical winners, records and facts share one daily rotating archive feature; the brand gets one identity slide. The production guest deck reviewed during this release had eight slides, down from fifteen. Content changes can change that count.

Trade decisions now use the forward production estimate for player-value percentiles, optimized lineup changes, usable depth, roster cuts, hand-built deals, offers, counteroffers and before/after previews. Completed points remain in the retrospective league report; they are not credited to the receiving owner. Existing projection/current-season/recent-form blending and DFL scoring remain shared. The availability adjustment discounts assumed missed games over the remaining calendar weeks, so the same absence matters more late in the season.

The ticket explains selected-player data support separately from value balance. Missing projections and production produce REVIEW rather than a decisive verdict, stop unsupported suggestions, and mark new completed-trade snapshots for review. Production-only fallbacks and injury assumptions are disclosed. Model coverage counts actual season projections rather than zero-valued fallback fields. New receipts carry `dflyzer-trade-v2-forward`; existing immutable receipts retain their original snapshot.

The estimates are remaining-season averages. They do not model specific future opponents, playoff schedules, bye-week lineup replacements, exact injury return dates or acceptance probabilities. Individual value ratings are relative to rostered players and draft-market signals, not a calibrated external trade price.
