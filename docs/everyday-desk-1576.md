# Everyday DFL desk — 1.576.0

Home shows one current roster decision beneath the matchup, followed by the
broadcast and existing league sections. Chicago weekday copy gives waivers,
practice and lineup checks a familiar place. Actual live NFL play overrides
that copy; the time between games does not claim football is live. The first
broadcast card remains independent, and the wall stays last.

The daily link refreshes in place and keeps keyboard focus. Fresh owned bench
players, actual kickoff locks and slot eligibility take priority over shopping.
IR/taxi depth and injured skill players cannot become cached streamer drops.
Trade targets must still belong to the stated owner and provide a meaningful
current projected gain. Refresh failures remove old advice from the action.

Trades includes a Targets disclosure, player search and Watch actions in offer
menus. Targets follow current ownership, with unavailable or season-ending
players prevented from becoming automatic offer targets. A target can reopen
its owner's search. Position filters persist; fresh visits still search all
teams. Targets/preferences are local to the current device, profile, season
and selected team. They do not publish trade intentions to other managers.

Saved deals retain their original player value/availability baseline and flag
material changes when loaded with current data. Changed ownership invalidates
a saved package. Current team names and existing Fair/Aggressive/Steal grading
remain authoritative. These flags are in the desk, not new phone alerts.

Fresh actual player scores can produce carrying-performance stories, and
close live matchups can produce a live-decimal story. No projected points are
presented as final. Existing event limits keep the broadcast from repeating
these on every refresh.

New disclosures share one finite reversible height animation. Background
advice updates retain control identity, motion-off/Reduced Motion stay static,
loading has status semantics, and shared error screens offer Retry and Home.
No new network polling, remote schema, paid feed or continuous effects.
