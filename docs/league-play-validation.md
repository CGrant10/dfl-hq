# DFL league play — v1.262.0

Facts now supports owner-ID personal filters, search, record/rivalry/championship filters, personal peak scores, deduplicated personal rivalry facts, portraits, per-fact branded sharing and 12-card progressive disclosure. The daily fact and weekly history remain available.

The optional weekly trivia panel loads only when opened. Five frozen questions use verified prior-season games, with varied winner/score/season prompts. Server grading creates one immutable attempt per selected member per week. Public column permissions exclude answers and other members' receipts. Own answer explanations become available after submission. Weekly/season leaderboards share ties. New seasons seed automatically; a subsequent league sync retries when historical data arrived later.

Clubhouse matchup cards include a pregame rivalry dossier: prior series record, last meeting, largest margin, winning streak and branded sharing. Current/future weeks do not contaminate pregame history. Predictions allow one matchup winner or league high scorer per selected member, editable until the first NFL kickoff, opening at the weekly Tuesday boundary. A fallback league calendar lock applies if the NFL slate is unavailable. Calls appear as pending until the week is final; tied high scorers share credit, tied matchup results have no winner. The recap contains receipts and a season prediction leaderboard. Existing selected-member identity applies, as with Wall and ballots.

Analyzer's lineup lab checks roster ownership, uniqueness, dedicated position coverage and flex eligibility. It compares one hypothetical bench swap against the existing lineup and the scheduled fantasy opponent. Missing projections remain unknown. Unavailable incoming players are rejected. Near-equal projections are identified as close calls. Changes never alter the Sleeper lineup. Players already playing/final carry a hypothetical comparison note. The six-week strategy rotation provides general lessons with real roster context, weekly projection fetch time and stale-cache status; it does not invent floor/ceiling probabilities. Shared data sources retain their own refresh TTLs.

Clubhouse adds themed loading placeholders, consistent shared cards, links to trivia and strategy, and restrained results animation respecting reduced motion. All new module dependencies are included in the offline shell. Schema source is in `clubhouse_play_schema.sql` and `clubhouse_call_results_schema.sql`; the latter includes the complete-score guard deployed in the follow-up migration.
