# Shared-image Medicine Wheel palette — v1.284.0

All 13 exported image variants use the fixed Medicine Wheel palette regardless of the sender's app theme: warm charcoal surfaces, warm white text, red and yellow brand fills, and lifted red for readable text. Old red/blue brand rules were replaced in profile, keepers, sportsbook tickets and aftermath, trades, weekly aftermath and lore. Lore now uses the central palette instead of its own cool gray colors. Golf scorecards now use the same dark surfaces and yellow/red score markers while retaining birdie/eagle circles and bogey/double squares. Golf leaderboards use shared card surfaces; golf share defaults translate the old generated blue marker into charcoal without rewriting stored team colors or changing the app's team palette.

Original crest artwork and custom team identities remain intact. Shared-image branding is independent of live theme selection. Canvas dimensions, data, labels and share/download fallback behavior are preserved.

Validation: pnpm check passed type checks, identifier checks, 1,024 tests across 113 files and build. Tests cover shared marker mapping and WCAG AA contrast for body/status inks on all card surfaces and score marker fills. Chromium rendered and encoded all 13 PNG variants: lore, profile, trade, keeper board, sportsbook ticket, sportsbook aftermath, golf board, teams, matchup poster, scorecard, leaderboard, weekly aftermath and Clubhouse recap. Each PNG is byte-identical under Dark, Light, Medicine Wheel, Medicine Wheel Light and Fairway. No legacy red/blue gradient stops remain. Full gallery and representative image artifacts reviewed.
